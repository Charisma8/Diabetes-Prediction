How to measure it yourself

Step-by-step — no equipment needed

1

Stand relaxed with your arm hanging at your side naturally.

2

Find the back of your upper arm (tricep), midway between your shoulder tip and elbow.

3

Using thumb and forefinger, gently pinch the skin vertically. Pinch only skin — not muscle.

4

Measure the thickness of the fold using a ruler in millimetres. Typical range: 12–35 mm.

No ruler? Use the estimator on the left. Women average 20–28 mm, Men average 14–22 mm.

High Risk — Take action now.
Your risk score puts you in the high-risk category. The good news: lifestyle changes can reduce this risk by 50–60% within 6 months.
✅

Eat daily

  • Leafy greens — spinach, methi, palak
  • Whole grains — brown rice, oats, ragi, jowar
  • Lentils & legumes — dal, rajma, chana
  • Fatty fish — mackerel, sardines (2× week)
  • Almonds, walnuts — small handful daily
  • Cinnamon in chai or food — lowers glucose
❌

Strictly avoid

  • White rice, maida, refined flour breads
  • Sugary drinks — cola, packaged juice, sweet chai
  • Deep fried foods — samosa, vada, chips daily
  • Ultra-processed biscuits and snacks
  • Mango, banana, grapes in excess
  • Alcohol — raises blood sugar directly

Plate method (every meal)

  • ½ plate: non-starchy vegetables
  • ¼ plate: lean protein (eggs, dal, fish, paneer)
  • ¼ plate: complex carbs (brown rice, chapati)
  • Eat 3 meals + 1–2 small snacks per day
  • Never skip breakfast — causes glucose spikes
  • Dinner at least 2 hours before sleeping

Morning routine

Moderate Risk — Start changing now.
Small, consistent changes now can prevent diabetes entirely. You have time — use it.
🍽️

Key diet changes

  • Switch white rice to brown rice or millets
  • Eat a protein-rich breakfast every single day
  • Cut out sugary drinks completely
  • Reduce fried snacks to once a week maximum
  • Add one extra vegetable serving to every meal
  • Eat whole fruit instead of juice
🚶

Start with walking

  • 30 min brisk walk every day to start
  • Walk 10 min after every main meal
  • Use stairs instead of lifts always
  • 7,000 steps/day reduces risk by 40%
🩺

Get tested now

  • Fasting blood sugar test — do this month
  • HbA1c test — shows 3-month average
  • Share your DPF score with your doctor
  • Consult a nutritionist for a meal plan
🎉
Great news — you are at low risk!
Here is how to keep it this way for life.

🥗 Diet habits to keep

  • Continue balanced meals — don't skip
  • Whole grains, vegetables, and protein at every meal
  • Limit added sugar to under 25g per day
  • Fibre-rich foods help blood sugar stability
  • Stay hydrated — water throughout the day

🏃 Activity level to maintain

  • Keep exercising at least 150 min per week
  • Walk after meals whenever possible
  • Avoid sitting for long periods without moving
  • Include both cardio and strength training
  • 20 min yoga daily is very protective long-term

🔬 Screenings to do regularly

  • Annual fasting blood glucose test
  • HbA1c check every 1–2 years after age 35
  • Blood pressure check every 6 months
  • BMI monitoring — keep between 18.5–23 (India)

⚠️ Warning signs to watch for

  • Increased thirst or frequent urination
  • Unexplained fatigue or blurred vision
  • Slow-healing wounds or repeated infections
  • Rapid weight gain, especially around abdomen
  • See a doctor immediately if any appear
